Penguins

A little bit about penguins

Penguins are flightless seabirds found almost exclusively in the Southern Hemisphere, highly adapted for diving and swimming with streamlined bodies and paddle-like flippers

The Penguin Family

More about Penguins

While associated with Antarctica, they live in diverse climates—from ice to tropical beaches—feeding on fish, krill, and squid. There are 18 species, ranging from the large Emperor penguin to the small Little penguin.
